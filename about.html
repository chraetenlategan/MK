About Michelle Kukkuk Beauty Lash & Body Salon

In 2015, we opened our doors with a dream and a passion for making women feel beautiful and confident. At the time, we could never have imagined what those first steps would grow into. Today, we look back with incredibly grateful hearts at how far we have come and feel truly blessed by every client who has trusted us, supported us and been part of our journey.


Limelight joined us in 2015 too, and over the years has grown and evolved alongside Michelle Kukkuk Beauty Lash & Body Salon. Today, Limelight forms the nail section of MK, beautifully complementing the salon and allowing us to offer our clients even more under one roof. Limelight is proudly run by Chrizelle Roberts, whose passion and expertise have made it such a special part of our salon family.


With almost 20 years of experience in the beauty industry, Michelle Kukkuk Beauty Lash & Body Salon has grown into a space where beauty, self-care, professional expertise and genuine care come together.


We offer a carefully selected range of professional beauty, skincare, lash, body and advanced aesthetic treatments, using trusted professional products and modern technology. Every treatment is tailored to the individual, because we believe that every client and every skin is unique.


For us, it has always been about more than simply providing a treatment. We want every woman who walks through our doors to feel welcomed, cared for, listened to and confident that she is in experienced hands.


We are passionate about quality, client safety, natural-looking results and continuous education, and we are constantly growing and evolving so that we can offer our clients the very best.


From the little salon we opened in 2015 to where we are today, we could never have dreamed of the journey that lay ahead. We are so incredibly grateful and blessed for every client, every friendship, every lesson and every person who has helped shape MK into what it is today.


Thank you for being part of our story.


Your beauty. Your confidence. Our passion.
